The problem

Your best moments are buried in 4-hour VODs.

More and more streamers get discovered through short clips, not the browse page. But making clips is a second job, and you already have one.

Scrubbing VODs takes hours

Finding the 40 seconds that matter in a long stream, then cutting, reframing and captioning it, can eat a whole day off.

Four apps, four uploads

TikTok, Shorts, Reels and X all want their own upload, caption and posting time. Doing that every day burns people out.

Posting stops, growth stops

Short-form rewards posting steadily. It's easy to post in bursts, then go quiet for weeks when life gets busy.

AI clipping apps help you find moments, but you still do the editing, approving and posting. VodLift does all of it for you.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Is this AI, or a real person?

Both. Our pipeline shortlists likely highlights from your VOD (loudness, talking, pace, transcript), then a human editor picks the best moments, fixes the cut and captions, and checks every clip before it's scheduled.

How was the demo on this page made?

We took a public stream recording released under Creative Commons (CC BY-SA 4.0) and ran it through the same open-source clipper we use as a first pass. The three clips and all four formats came straight out of it with no manual edits. It's stock footage, not a client or our own channel. Full credits are in CREDITS.md.

Do you post on my accounts?

Yes, through Postiz. You connect your TikTok, YouTube, Instagram and X accounts to your own Postiz workspace (a couple of clicks each), and clips are scheduled from there. You can require approval before anything goes live, and you can disconnect at any time.

Who owns the clips?

You do. Clips are made from your content and belong to you. You can download every MP4 whenever you like, including after you cancel.

What about copyrighted game music?

We lower or remove licensed music we spot in the clips and avoid moments that rely on it. You're still responsible for following each game's and platform's content rules. Tell us about anything to avoid, like DMCA-sensitive streams.

Which games and streams work best?

Anything with reactions and commentary: shooters, battle royales, horror, competitive, variety and Just Chatting. Facecam streams give the most layout options, but we can cut gameplay-only streams too.

How fast do I get clips?

Within 48 hours of receiving your VOD on Starter and Growth, and within 24 hours on Pro. The free sample is delivered within 48 hours.

Can I cancel?

Anytime, with no contracts. Your plan runs until the end of the billing month, and you keep everything we've made.

Do I need to self-host Postiz?

No. Your plan includes a hosted Postiz workspace that we set up for you. If you already self-host Postiz, we can schedule into your instance instead.
